About YouTube Music Recommender

Welcome to the YouTube Music Recommender! This application uses advanced machine learning algorithms to analyze your music preferences and recommend new songs you'll love.

How It Works

1. Audio Feature Extraction

We extract 11 key audio features from each song, including danceability, energy, tempo, valence (happiness), acousticness, and more. These features are analyzed using sophisticated audio processing algorithms.

2. Similarity Analysis

Our recommendation engine uses cosine similarity to find songs that match your taste. It compares the audio features of songs you like with our database to find the best matches.

3. Diversity Algorithm

To prevent repetitive recommendations, we've implemented a diversity algorithm that ensures your playlist has variety while still matching your taste.

Audio Features Analyzed

  • Danceability: How suitable a track is for dancing
  • Energy: Intensity and activity measure
  • Valence: Musical positiveness (happy vs sad)
  • Tempo: Speed of the music in BPM
  • Acousticness: Whether the track is acoustic
  • Instrumentalness: Predicts if track contains vocals
  • Speechiness: Presence of spoken words
  • Liveness: Detects audience presence
  • Key & Mode: Musical key and major/minor scale
  • Loudness: Overall volume of the track

Technology Stack

  • Backend: Python, Flask
  • Audio Analysis: librosa, soundfile
  • Machine Learning: scikit-learn
  • Data Processing: pandas, numpy
  • YouTube API: Google API Python Client
  • Audio Download: yt-dlp

Privacy & Data

Your playlist data is only used temporarily to generate recommendations and is not stored. All processing happens on our server, and we don't share your data with third parties.

Tips for Best Results

  • Use playlists with at least 10-20 songs for better recommendations
  • Adjust the diversity slider based on how adventurous you want to be
  • Try different playlists to explore various music styles
  • Use the mood discovery feature when you want something specific
